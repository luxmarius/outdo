This was the trip that started it all. The idea was simple: leave a big city by bike and not stop until we reached somewhere that felt like the opposite of it.

We rolled out of Munich in September with roughly 320 km and 6,400 m of climbing between us and Lake Garda. The Alps don't care about your training plan, and by day two Reschenpass had made that very clear.

## What we'd do differently

Pack less. Book the Merano hotel earlier. Start the day-four climb before the sun is fully up.

## What we'd do again

Everything else.
