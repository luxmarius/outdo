Every year the same question: where next? Here's how we settle it.

## Start from a city with an airport, end somewhere you'd want to stay

Flying in and out of the same place forces a loop. Point-to-point is more satisfying and gives the trip a direction.

## One big feature

A mountain range, a coastline, a river. Something that shapes the whole route rather than a list of nice towns.

## Nobody has ridden it

If one of us knows the roads, the surprise is gone. The best days have been the ones where we had no idea what was around the corner.
