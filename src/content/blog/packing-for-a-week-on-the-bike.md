After a few years of doing this we've stopped arguing about packing. The list below is what we carry for a five to seven day trip with hotel nights. No tent, no stove.

## On the bike

- Handlebar bag: rain jacket, gloves, snacks for the day
- Frame bag: tools, tube, pump, charger, powerbank
- Saddle bag: everything for the evening

## Clothes

Two sets of riding kit, one set of evening clothes that pack small, and a puffer that doubles as a pillow on the train home. You will wash bibs in a hotel sink. Accept it.

## The one thing people forget

A small dry bag for your phone and passport. Alpine weather turns fast and a wet passport at a border is not a fun story to tell.
